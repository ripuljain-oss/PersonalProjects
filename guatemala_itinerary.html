Morning Kayak on Lake Atitlán
Rent kayaks directly from the hotel. Go early — by midmorning, winds pick up and the lake gets choppy. The crystal-clear water reflects three volcanoes — one of the most beautiful morning activities imaginable. Kids ages 10 & 12 are perfect for this!

📋 Practical Tips for the Jain Family

💰 Money
Currency: Guatemalan Quetzal (GTQ). ~7.7 GTQ per $1 USD. Withdraw cash at airport ATM. Many places add 5% for cards.
🚗 Getting Around
In Antigua: Uber or tuk-tuk. City to city: private shuttle (safest for families). Lake Atitlán: lanchas (public boats, ~25Q/person/ride).
🌡️ Weather
Late March = dry season. Antigua ~22°C/72°F. Lake Atitlán slightly cooler with afternoon breezes. Sunscreen is essential!
🥗 Food Safety
In Antigua & Atitlán, tourist restaurants use filtered water for ice and cooking. Stick to bottled water. No tap water.
👟 What to Pack
Comfortable walking shoes (cobblestones!), swimwear for the lake, light layers for evenings, bug spray, sunscreen, cash.
🗣️ Language Tips
Spanish is official. A warm "buenos días" goes a long way. Locals are incredibly friendly and welcoming to families.
🔒 Safety
Antigua & Atitlán are generally safe for tourists. Avoid night travel, use reputable transportation. Stick to well-traveled areas.
📱 Book Ahead
Pre-book: ChocoMuseo workshop, private shuttles, cooking class, and Iximché tour. Casa del Mundo fills up — confirm your reservation!
